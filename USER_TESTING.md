# LogiMatch User Testing Protocol

## Goal

Validate that seekers and providers can complete the Milestone 3 flows without guidance: bidding, accepting, manifest download, analytics review, and core dashboard navigation.

## Participants

Test with 4-6 participants:

- 2-3 acting as service seekers
- 2-3 acting as logistics providers

## Tasks

### Seeker Tasks

1. Register and verify an account.
2. Post a freight job with dimensions, risk flags, schedule windows, and target budget.
3. Review the recommended price range.
4. Compare incoming bids.
5. Accept the preferred bid.
6. Download the freight manifest PDF.
7. Open the profile insights tab and explain what the summary cards mean.

### Provider Tasks

1. Register and verify an account.
2. Filter the open market by route or freight requirements.
3. Expand a job manifest.
4. Submit a bid.
5. View accepted dispatch details after winning.
6. Download the freight manifest PDF.
7. Open the profile insights tab and explain win rate or earnings.

## Data To Record

For each task, record:

- Success or failure
- Time taken
- Confusing labels or screens
- Bugs or broken states
- Ease-of-use rating from 1 to 5
- Suggested improvement

## Acceptance Target

Aim for:

- At least 80% task completion without facilitator help.
- No critical blocker in posting jobs, bidding, accepting bids, downloading manifests, or viewing insights.
- At least two UI improvements applied based on test feedback.
